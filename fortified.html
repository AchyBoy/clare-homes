Built for the storm

When severe weather strikes, a FORTIFIED Roof keeps the wind and rain out

It prevents a cascade of damage that can destroy the home and belongings. The FORTIFIED Roof model was specifically designed to prevent damage that commonly occurs during:

  • High winds
  • Hurricanes
  • Hailstorms
  • Severe thunderstorms
  • Tornadoes up to EF-2

This roof assembly is the key to making Clare Homes resilient! We are certified FORTIFIED Professional Contractors.
