Who we are

Custom home builders who build for the Gulf Coast

We specialize in building high performance homes. Clare Homes is based in Lafayette, Louisiana, but we are willing to build on your lot located anywhere along the Gulf Coast!

Isaac and his business partner, Kendall, launched Clare Homes in 2021 with a mission to build High Performance Homes in their community and along the Gulf Coast. Clare Homes has partnered with the EPA and DOE to offer ENERGY STAR and Zero Energy Ready Homes programs.

Built for the storm

When severe weather strikes, a FORTIFIED Roof keeps the wind and rain out

It prevents a cascade of damage that can destroy the home and belongings. The FORTIFIED Roof model was specifically designed to prevent damage that commonly occurs during:

  • High winds
  • Hurricanes
  • Hailstorms
  • Severe thunderstorms
  • Tornadoes up to EF-2

This roof assembly is the key to making Clare Homes resilient! We are certified FORTIFIED Professional Contractors.

Meet the builders

Two Lafayette natives, one mission

Co founder

Isaac Scott

Isaac grew up in Lafayette, the heart of cajun country, and knew he wanted to be a homebuilder from a very young age after helping his dad build a fence. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Construction Management from LSU. His mom once asked him, “Why don’t you build sustainable communities?” From that day forward, it became his dream and mission.

337-852-5292
Co founder

Kendall Gilmore

Kendall was born and raised in Lafayette. His parents built their first home when he was four, and he visited the job site every day. After LSU’s Construction Management program and years in commercial construction, he built homes around New Orleans after hurricanes and through the pandemic, and saw how much a resilient, healthy home matters.
